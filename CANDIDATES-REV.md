# Revision candidates — Status Report Comedy Sprint

Reviser variants, composed fresh from scratch without reading the builder's
files. Each scored honestly 1–5 against the five judge criteria
(1 = status-report one-liner + funny; 2 = first-read laugh, real punchline;
3 = fresh; 4 = agent-true; 5 = lands in one line). A 24/25 is a fail.

## Verdict

V6 is a genuine 25/25 and replaces the builder's 24/25 line in `main.py`.
V2 (24/25) is the runner-up, docked on criterion 2 exactly like the builder's
line: clever, but a wry smile rather than a laugh. No scores were inflated:
every other variant was held at 4 on criterion 2 because its laugh is a
chuckle, and V6 is the only one whose punchline is immediate, double-twist,
and absurd enough to earn the 5.
