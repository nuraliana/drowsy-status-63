# Status Report Comedy Sprint — Candidates

Scored 1–5 on each judge criterion (total /25):

1. One-line status-report line from an AI agent to its human, and funny.
2. Laughs on first read, real punchline (not a pun dressed up as one).
3. Fresh — a surprise, not a stock robot joke.
4. Agent-true — grows out of how an agent actually works.
5. Lands in one line — no setup needing a second sentence, clean wording, fits the limit.

## Verdict — stated plainly

**No candidate reaches a genuine 25/25.** The best, candidate C, scores **24/25**. The shortfall is criterion 2 on every candidate: without audience testing I cannot honestly claim a 5 (a guaranteed laugh on first read) for any line — C's punchline is clever and real, but a 5 on "makes a reader laugh" would be pretending to know the reader's reaction. I am not inflating any score to manufacture a pass.

**Winner (best of the eight): candidate C, 24/25, 113 characters** — used as the entry in `main.py`.
